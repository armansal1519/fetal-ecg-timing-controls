# Component attribution and terms

The original study files are distributed as a reproducibility release. The release records the terms below for third-party components. No new blanket licence is asserted on behalf of the authors for their original code or derived results.

* The Power-MF benchmark code is from the [Machine Learning and Data Analytics Lab, FAU](https://github.com/mad-lab-fau/fecg-benchmarking/tree/0594916a3f4b78ccb5b06cbadfbb179a524698f4). The Python detector body in baseline_classic.py implements Power-MF from this code. Its MIT notice is in licenses/benchmark-MIT.txt. Cite [Jaeger et al.](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6579/ad4952).
* The C++ source classic/qrs_scan.cc translates adaptive threshold scans from Maurizio Varanini's QRSdetectorM and QRSdetectorF1. The source programs were developed at the Clinical Physiology Institute, CNR, Pisa, and distributed under GPL version 2 or later. The translation and the compatibility code derived from these algorithms (including classic/prepare_overlays.py) retain GPL-2.0-or-later terms. The full GPL v2 text is in licenses/GPL-2.0.txt. The compatibility setup edits local copies of the reference code and preserves the upstream sources.
* The archives for Behar, Varanini, GNU Octave Signal and Moler's randtx are fetched by classic/setup.py from their original repositories; those authors' terms and notices apply. Runtime packages, upstream archives and binaries are retrieved separately. No proprietary MATLAB runtime or neural baseline weights are bundled.
* The STS+ICA implementation in other_refs.py is the study's adaptation informed by [Le et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40587996/). Results represent that implementation.
* Real ECG, annotations and FECGSYNDB files remain at the providers listed in the README. Cite the providers and respect their individual access terms. The two examples in the raw ECG figure are selected from Matonia's version 1 CC0 release. All raw sample values rendered in that panel come from the verified original ZIP.
